About

I am currently a PhD student in Pure Mathematics at the University of Alberta , under the supervision of Vladyslav Yaskin and Beatrice-Helen Vritsiou. I also completed my Master's degree at the University of Alberta under their supervision.

My research interests include convex geometry, combinatorial geometry and geometric tomography.
